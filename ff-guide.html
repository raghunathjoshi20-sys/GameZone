🎮 About Free Fire

Free Fire is a battle royale game where players compete to survive. Good decisions, teamwork, and practice can help you improve.

🪂 Landing Tips

  • Choose a landing location that matches your skill level.
  • Look for useful equipment before taking fights.
  • Learn the map and plan a safe route.

🎯 Aim and Combat Tips

  • Practice aiming in training mode.
  • Use cover instead of standing in the open.
  • Try different weapons to find what suits you.
  • Adjust sensitivity gradually and test your settings.

🛡️ Survival Tips

  • Watch the safe zone and move before time runs out.
  • Carry suitable healing items.
  • Use communication to coordinate with teammates.
  • Learn from each match instead of rushing every fight.

🏆 Beginner Advice

Focus on learning movement, aiming, positioning, and teamwork. Improvement comes with practice and patience.
