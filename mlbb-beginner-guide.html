Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Beginner's Guide

Welcome to GameZone! This guide will help new players understand the basics of Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB).

1. Understand the Goal

Your main objective is to work with your team to destroy the enemy base. Defeating enemies can help, but turrets and teamwork are also important.

2. Learn the Hero Roles

  • Tank: Protects teammates and absorbs damage.
  • Fighter: Balances damage and durability.
  • Assassin: Often targets vulnerable enemies.
  • Mage: Uses magic damage and special abilities.
  • Marksman: Often deals sustained ranged damage.
  • Support: Helps teammates with healing, protection, or crowd control.

3. Learn Your Lane

EXP Lane is often suited to fighters. Gold Lane helps certain heroes earn gold. Mid Lane provides opportunities to help other lanes. Junglers clear monsters and contest objectives, while roamers support teammates around the map.

4. Five Tips for Beginners

  1. Learn your hero's skills before ranked matches.
  2. Watch the minimap regularly.
  3. Protect your turrets and clear minion waves.
  4. Choose items that fit your hero and opponents.
  5. Work with your team instead of fighting alone.
GameZone tip: Focus on learning one skill at a time. Practice makes it easier to understand the game.

Conclusion

MLBB is about strategy, teamwork, and practice. Learn your role, protect objectives, and keep improving after every match.
